Our Story

About Miacher Kho

Miacher Kho is a mountain campsite in Nagar Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan. It sits in a landscape shaped by the Karakoram, with views that include the presence of Rakaposhi and the surrounding peaks.

The experience is intentionally simple: tents, open sky, mountain light, and the quiet that comes from being away from the noise of cities. Guests come for the scenery, the night sky, and a slower pace.

We focus on providing a safe, welcoming base for travelers who want authentic outdoor living rather than a resort. Facilities are practical. The setting is the main reason people return.

Whether you arrive as a couple, a family, a group of friends, or a university team, the goal is the same — to let the mountains do the talking.
