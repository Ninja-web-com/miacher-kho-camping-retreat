What You Can Do

Experiences

The setting itself is the main activity. Here are some of the ways guests spend their time.

Hiking & Viewpoint Walks

Short walks and longer explorations around the campsite open new angles on the valley and peaks. Suitable for most fitness levels with guidance available.

Stargazing

Away from city lights the sky is remarkably clear. Many guests say the night sky is one of the strongest memories of their stay.

Bonfire Evenings

Gather around the fire after sunset. Food, conversation, and the mountains as a backdrop.

Mountain Photography

Sunrise and sunset light on Rakaposhi and the surrounding peaks make for powerful images. Photographers often plan extra time around golden hour.
